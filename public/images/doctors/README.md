# Doctor portraits

Upload approved doctor photos to this folder.

Recommended: portrait WebP or JPG, approximately 800 × 1000 pixels or higher, consistent framing and good lighting.

Use lowercase descriptive filenames such as `doctor-01.webp`, `doctor-02.webp` and `doctor-03.webp`.

Publish only doctor names, photographs and qualifications that have been verified and authorized for use.

These images will be available in Next.js at `/images/doctors/<filename>` after deployment.
